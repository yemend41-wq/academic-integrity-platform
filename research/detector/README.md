# Academic Integrity Platform · Writing-signal research

The production candidate has **failed the false-positive target**. The assessment build serves AIP-HL-3 strictly as an experimental review signal. It applies separate local logistic regressions to overlapping English or Arabic text windows. All model weights are learned. The software emits no score for a document with fewer than 48 eligible words. It may also abstain on windows with mixed scripts. Its language routing distinguishes Arabic-script from Latin-script inputs and does not itself identify every Latin-script language. Only English and Arabic were evaluated. A blue span marks a model observation for discussion with the writer. It cannot establish authorship.



**Assessment-build results for the active AIP-HL-3 operating point.** On English human texts the numeric display at 20% or above triggered for 3/393 in the Liang cohorts, 5/555 on MAGE/RAID and 0/399 on UKP. On Arabic it triggered on 227/799 in the independent Arabic collection, 56/400 Ar-APT press articles, 32/701 human academic abstracts, and 35/358 ANAD 2021 articles. Inclusive counts also include a suppressed positive observation and appear in the live card. Production release is false. HL-3 reduced measured false alarms on the previously recorded external cohorts, while Arabic machine detection also declined. The stricter HL-4 was rejected for inadequate sensitivity. The independent committee evaluation remains outstanding.

## Training and scoring

The features are sublinearly scaled, L2-normalized hashed words, adjacent word pairs, and prefixes/suffixes. The score assigned to each sentence (or piece of an unusually long sentence) comes from its nearest 96-word window center. Windows move in 32-word steps; samples shorter than 96 eligible words use a single window. The final report gives the share of eligible words in flagged units. A positive share strictly below 20% produces only `*%` and withholds span highlights and the underlying flagged-word count. Zero and insufficient input are separate states. No input text is transmitted by this component.

The baseline corpus contains 742 distinct documents after filtering. The frozen training split contains 336 Arabic and 168 English texts. Arabic vocalization copies are discarded. Arabic human sources and machine prompt families form split groups; English pair members share the split of their essay topic. The training loss weights documents and classes so long essays with many windows do not dominate. Test labels were held back from coefficient fitting and threshold selection. Few calibration documents, related passages, educational mismatch and prompt/topic skew limit transfer between corpora. Scores on these internal holdouts cannot substitute for external validation.

## Independent outcomes

- The English baseline signalled 18 of 393 eligible human texts in the Liang et al. cohorts. Twelve were disclosed as numeric alerts at 20% or above. Individual groups differed substantially: TOEFL 0/90, US eighth-grade essays 0/88, college essays 2/70, scientific abstracts 16/145. One TOEFL item was excluded for overlap. The small TOEFL sample cannot establish fairness.
- The separate Arabic course-project collection yielded signals on 449 of 799 eligible human-labelled documents (444 numerically visible). It detected 759 of 812 eligible AI-labelled examples. One overlapping human item and 95 short or insufficient cases were excluded; see the per-class counts. The study authors’ labels were used as supplied. The population includes material outside the AIRABIC training domain.
- In a separate cross-domain English check on MAGE/RAID, AIP-HL-1 signalled 90/555 human texts and 493/1,573 machine texts. A second English classifier learned from a domain-diverse development set, then signalled 9/555 and 597/1,573. Both missed the 1% ceiling using the inclusive endpoint that counts a suppressed observation. The challenger also missed 50% minimum recall and was not released for production. ELLIPSE/ICNALE learner cohorts had 1/328 signals with that challenger. The test split was inspected once for the predeclared decision. The small Liang cohorts are reported separately.
- A third candidate added the original Arabic Wikipedia passages published as ARCD in 2019 to train/calibrate the Arabic component. Documents were grouped by article and the same conservative operating rule was fixed before external evaluation. On new held-out Ar-APT articles, its false alerts fell from 130/400 to 59/400 and recall from 298/359 to 263/359. The academic-abstract false alerts fell from 189/701 to 33/701. The article and academic false-positive ceilings still failed, and sensitivity declined. AIP-HL-3 also failed the production gate. It is enabled only as an explicitly experimental review signal in the assessment build. Separate GPT-4-polished press and Claude-polished academic cohorts are reported as machine-assisted text and are excluded from binary authorship performance. See `arabic-challenger-decision.json` and the per-domain records.
- A fourth operating point reused the previous external human test cohorts explicitly as calibration. Its frozen threshold was the most conservative 99.5th percentile of per-document maximum human margin across those source groups, retaining the same 48-word eligibility floor. In new sources it triggered on 0/399 eligible UKP persuasive essays (one-sided 95% upper 0.75%) and 0/358 eligible ANAD 2021 news articles (upper 0.83%). It also missed 337/350 English machine essays and all 100 eligible Arabic machine-origin translated task/answer pairs. Another 122 Arabic pairs were too short or ineligible. This research threshold materially sacrifices detection and was rejected. The Arabic cohorts do not establish a rate on native student essays, and repeated publisher, author and prompt effects limit binomial interpretations. The earlier audits still describe the earlier frozen versions only.
- Controlled mixed documents splice contiguous blocks of real held-out human and generated writing at 25%, 50% and 75% of counted words. The same source pairs recur at different ratios. Check `metrics.json` for each language and share. This is a constructed localization test, distinct from mixed student essays independently collected in the field.
- English rewriting and translation were tested separately. The archived baseline signalled 13/31 rewritten generated college essays and 49/145 rewritten generated abstracts from Liang et al. Of 96 distinct published English–German–English round-trip spans derived from human-source Wikipedia text, 55 were eligible and 3 signalled. Machine translation of human prose has ambiguous authorship under a binary human/machine label. The translation result is descriptive. Resilience is unproven.
- For cross-source checks, the code excludes matching hashes within the cohort or text with at least 20% exact seven-word shingle containment against the development corpus, as defined in the relevant audit script. This filter will miss semantic duplicates. Some underlying prompts and authors can recur. Confidence limits shown are one-sided 95% binomial limits under independent sampling; the finite, grouped samples do not provide a population guarantee.

## Reproduce

From this directory, install `requirements-research.txt` in a separate Python environment. Node.js 22+ is sufficient for JavaScript scoring. Use an outside scratch directory for all source text and temporary features. Acquiring inputs requires outbound access to the cited public GitHub repositories.

```sh
python fetch_data.py /tmp/aip-model-data
python reproduce.py /tmp/aip-model-data
```

The downloader validates Git blob hashes and file SHA-256 digests for each input. The reproduction script rebuilds the baseline, two coefficient challengers and the rejected high-specificity operating point, verifies coefficient hashes, and compares thirteen regenerated metric/calibration artifacts. The repository contains the active experimental model and archived baseline, scripts, source digests, per-document hashed held-out outcomes, source-labelled aggregate studies, exact training versions and release decision. Raw documents remain outside the checkout.

The final academic acceptance decision requires a separate committee-controlled study on unseen essays covering document length, author backgrounds, modern generators and calibrated mixed segments. These research evaluations do not meet that acceptance target.

## Release 4 independent-source experiment

The timestamped release-4 acceptance record refers to the unchanged experimental AIP-HL-3 detector. The HL-5 research challenger was rejected. Its Arabic component retrained on source-balanced AIRABIC, ARCD and historical Ar-APT data. Ar-APT therefore counts as development data for HL-5. A separate source (o-qn) furnished 741 eligible human calibration texts. The frozen protocol and sample digest are in `release4-protocol.json` and `release4-study.json`.

The new test used Claude-generated research abstracts from titles paired with the published originals in the [KFUPM corpus](https://github.com/KFUPM-JRCAI/arabic-text-detection), and the [ArguGPT out-of-distribution machine essays](https://github.com/huhailinguist/ArguGPT). The preparation script excludes overlapping historical sources and freezes topic pairs before fitting. It reserved 400 of the 3,000 Arabic pairs and 400 of 500 English machine texts. The public data omit consistent author identities. Source separation and text-overlap screening cannot certify author separation. There was no committee-supplied blind panel. Neither source is a representative population of enrolled students.

Among 400 eligible human Arabic abstracts, AIP-HL-3 flagged 34. It flagged 325/400 paired generated abstracts. HL-5 flagged 0/400 in each group. All four counts refer to visible and inclusive signals alike for these cohorts. The unchanged English component flagged 35/400 new generated essays, including 11 visible scores. The active model remains experimental. The site card includes two-sided Wilson intervals for eligible documents, abstentions, descriptive rates for assisted/translated text and mixture errors in percentage points. Historical cohorts can share authors and examples; never pool across the card.

In the earlier Arabic control data the press group had 18/25 visible alerts in texts of 384 words or more, compared with 20/172 at 96–191 words. Within the same 96–191-word band, course-project texts had 146/518 visible alerts and research abstracts had 22/444. Length and source are associated with different observed error rates; unknown author and genre effects preclude a causal explanation.

To reproduce the experiment, run the previous acquisition and reproduction steps, then run `python release4-reproduce.py /tmp/aip-model-data`. All processing uses local copies; the two new source and full-sample digests identify the inputs. No raw learner essays, dataset excerpts or unpublished evaluator material belong in the site repository.
